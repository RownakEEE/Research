# CMOS Analog & RF Circuit Design

**CMOS Analog & RF Circuit Design** explores integrated-circuit architectures and design techniques for power management, RF/millimeter-wave systems, and emerging communication and sensing applications. The research investigates circuit-level architectures, implementation techniques, and performance trade-offs across different CMOS technologies. The work spans **low-dropout voltage regulators (LDOs), low-noise amplifiers (LNAs), injection-locked frequency dividers (ILFDs), ring oscillators, RF front-end design, and circuit performance analysis** for IoT, millimeter-wave, and wireless systems.

---

## Research Highlights

- **CMOS LDO Design** — Analysis of analog, digital, and hybrid low-dropout voltage regulator architectures and their design trade-offs.
- **Power Management** — Investigation of dropout voltage, quiescent current, transient response, power-supply rejection, and efficiency.
- **CMOS LNA Design** — Analysis of low-noise amplifier architectures and circuit techniques for improving RF front-end performance.
- **RF & mmWave Circuits** — Investigation of CMOS circuit architectures for RF, SHF, and millimeter-wave transceiver applications.
- **Oscillator & Frequency-Divider Design** — Exploration of CMOS ring oscillators and LC-tank-based injection-locked frequency dividers.
- **Performance Analysis** — Comparative evaluation of gain, noise, linearity, bandwidth, power consumption, frequency range, and circuit-level design trade-offs.
