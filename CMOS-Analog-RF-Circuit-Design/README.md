# CMOS Analog & RF Circuit Design

This research focuses on CMOS analog and RF integrated circuit design for power management and wireless communication systems. The work investigates circuit architectures, design techniques, and performance trade-offs for low-dropout voltage regulators (LDOs) and low-noise amplifiers (LNAs).

The research spans CMOS circuit design, power management, RF front-end design, low-noise amplification, performance analysis, and emerging applications in IoT, millimeter-wave communication, and future wireless systems.

## Research Highlights

- **CMOS LDO Design:** Analysis of analog, digital, and hybrid low-dropout voltage regulator architectures and their design trade-offs.
- **Power Management:** Investigation of dropout voltage, quiescent current, transient response, power-supply rejection, and efficiency.
- **CMOS LNA Design:** Analysis of low-noise amplifier architectures and circuit techniques for improving RF front-end performance.
- **RF & mmWave Circuits:** Investigation of CMOS circuit architectures for RF and millimeter-wave communication systems.
- **Performance Analysis:** Comparative evaluation of gain, noise figure, linearity, bandwidth, power consumption, and circuit-level design trade-offs.
- **Applications:** Exploration of CMOS circuit technologies for IoT sensors, wireless transceivers, mmWave systems, and emerging communication applications.
